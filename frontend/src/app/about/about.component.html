RBC Portfolio Tracker is a self-directed investing platform for clients who prefer to manage their own registered and non-registered accounts. It consolidates your holdings, cash balances and asset mix in one view and lets you research, compare and order funds and other securities online. The platform provides access to a catalogue of funds, model portfolios and research material, along with order entry, trade confirmations, account statements and alerts. Orders placed through the trade ticket are executed under your own responsibility; the platform does not provide personalized investment advice. LINK_TERMS_OF_USE Client support is available through secure messaging, and documents such as statements and tax slips can be downloaded or archived from the document centre at any time. Accounts are protected by two-factor authentication and session monitoring.
